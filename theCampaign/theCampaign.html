The Story so far...

So far we only have four crew members. Our captain Phobos, our cook Cookie the Wookie, the engineer, only known as "The Engie" and finally Zaros, The Mask. Each of them respectively are the obvious engineer of the ship, the world renown cook of cookies, the daring and adventurous pilot Phobos, and the skilled shrewed diplomap. All of these characters are indentured servants to the benevolent slave trader Lincoln who pays them well for their services. Their most recent mission was to retrieve a shipment of glitteryll from Ryylothh, unfortunately the mission went south when their contact had no interest in providing the goods, the team fiercely fended off the attackers but were forced to make a crash landing due to damage to their ship.

Session 2...

After resting at Lincoln s place, the party heads out into the dark cold of the desert only to stumble upon an old cave containing an abandoned luxury spa and a small storage room where they d happened to find some old heavy armor and a metal shield! Unfortunately during his rejoicing Cookie tested his new shield out on the Engie breaking one of his ribs and they needed to rush back to the ship to attend to his medical needs. Later on the ship after The Engie is all healed up, the crew decide to head off for Ryylothh to pick up the Glyteryll under the guise of buying slaves. On their way, without eggs, Cookie and Zaros attempt to make cookies substituting milk for the eggs. Finally after almost a month "at sea" the party arrives at Ryylothh, looking at the rocky, windy, and varied planet. Still under Republic control, anxious about a Sith attack fleet that may be lurking nearby.

Session 3...

Upon reaching orbit of Ryylothh, the crew of the Lincoln 295 head for the northern mountainous ranges of Ryylothh to purchase Glyteryll. As a snow storm and harsh gusts batter the crew s ship. Upon entering the slave auction house they meet with Foliage Smith and the crew provides him the money as requested. Promptly leaving with a few purchased slaves and 50 credits lost on gambling, the crew headed to the drop off location. Minutes after taking off the crew were ambushed by four z95 Headhunters, after fending off and destroying two of them, the Sith fleet begins it s assault and annihilates the remaining fighters while thankfully missing the main characters. Unfortunately, the damage to the main engine on the Lincoln 295 forced them to make a crash landing. The only one left standing after the crash was the tough and durable wookie, Cookie.


The players

The Captain

Not much is known of Phobos past, although he is known to some as a genius pilot. After he became indentured to Lincoln he grew in renown. As the quickest, trickiest smuggler around. While he didn t always smuggle goods, his skills held a certain respect, and price. Some people in the galaxy would want to use him. Maybe some would want to get back at Lincoln.


The Cookie

Born to Dough and Chocolate, young Cookie s destiny was set the day he was born. However, his parents realized just how stupid and incompetent he was; once he was of age he was shipped off to a deserted planet. There he studied the way of the axe through his scavenging and creating simple tools.


The Engie

The origins of the Engie are unknown, but we do know he came from a human colony near the outer rim colonies. Quickly picking up skills in repairing damaged electrical and mechanical equipment he became very useful as an engineer later in his life. Realizing that the Candorian Plague would likely destroy his planet he took passage on the next ship out working as an engineer which eventually lead him to Tatooine


The Mask

Zaros came from a family who ran a college that started dabbling their money in casinos and lost. The only way to recuperate some of the profits they old their only son into slavery along with selling their college. Zaros has been a slave for 2 years before being rope into by force by his master to help a tag of motley cue do illegal things
