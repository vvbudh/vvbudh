This is how you install Teamspeak 6.

Download Teamspeak 6 for your OS. Do not download TS3. https://teamspeak.com/en/downloads/?product=ts6




Because you're on Windows still, Go to your Downloads folder and double click the teamspeak-client.msi, follow the instructions and when it’s completed launch the app.




Click create an account.




Type in your username, email, and a password that you haven’t used anywhere else. Then click Create Account.




If you need to, retype your username and password, and Sign In.




Once you’re logged in, click the plus sign next to bookmarks and type in "vvbudh.com"




Then click the new bookmark you’ve just added. That joins a server.




Once you’ve joined the server, you’ll be entered into the Lobby. Double click on any other channel to join it in RED. To exit the server click the X at the top left next to the server you’ve joined. The YELLOW. You now know how to use Teamspeak and the server I worked so hard to create on my Pentium 2 server.
